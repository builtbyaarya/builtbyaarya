# Aarya

Mechanical engineering student at USC Viterbi. I build open tools and data on the economics of energy hardware: what it costs to build solar and battery factories in the US, how import duties change that math, and why new factories get built or cancelled.

### Background
- Solar factory CapEx and OpEx modeling (Germany, 2025)
- Hydrogen fuel-cell lifecycle research (RWTH Aachen)

### Working on
- **US solar trade tracker:** an open table of US solar import duty rates and factory capacity, with a source for every number
- **A browser game** where you try to build a solar factory in the US and survive tariffs, tax credits and price crashes
